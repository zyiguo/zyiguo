01

Search Agent Post-Training ↗

A single-GPU post-training pipeline for a Qwen3-4B search agent: rule-based trajectory SFT, multi-question GRPO, and fixed-set evaluation. Uses a JSON search/answer protocol and local BM25 retrieval, with resumable checkpoints, experiment identity checks, and training telemetry.

An engineering adaptation of Search-R1 ideas, rather than a reproduction of the paper or an official HotpotQA benchmark submission.

Python · Qwen3-4B · LoRA · GRPO · BM25 · TensorBoard
Supervised fine-tuning / Search-agent training / Evaluation & telemetry
